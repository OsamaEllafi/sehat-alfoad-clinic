About Us

At Sehat Alfoad Cardiology Clinic, we combine cutting-edge technology with compassionate care. Our team of renowned cardiologists specializes in diagnosing and treating complex heart conditions, ensuring every patient receives personalized attention.

"We believe that a healthy heart is the foundation of a full life. Our mission is to provide the highest standard of cardiovascular care to our community."

Our Services

Consultations

Comprehensive cardiac evaluations by top specialists to diagnose and manage your heart health.

Echocardiograms

State-of-the-art ultrasound imaging to assess heart structure and function non-invasively.

Stress Testing

Advanced cardiovascular stress testing to evaluate blood flow and heart performance during physical activity.
